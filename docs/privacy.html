Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

AuraSolve is built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the extension reads, where it goes, and what we don't do.

What AuraSolve reads

AuraSolve only reads a page when you press one of its buttons, or while AutoPilot is running after you start it. It reads the current question: its text, its answer fields, and an image of the question area so diagrams and graphs can be understood. It doesn't read your browsing history, passwords, or other tabs.

Where that information goes

To produce an answer, the question text and image are sent to the AuraSolve service, which passes them to OpenAI and returns the result. The AuraSolve service doesn't save your questions, images, or answers. To prevent abuse, it keeps your IP address in memory for about a minute for rate limiting, then discards it. OpenAI processes API data under its own privacy policy and may keep it for a limited time for abuse monitoring.

What stays on your device

Your preferences are saved with Chrome's extension storage on your computer. Removing the extension deletes them.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or API keys.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies in the extension.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • No storing of your questions, answers, or grades.

This website

This site doesn't use analytics or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and checks GitHub for the latest download, so those services see a normal web request from your browser.

Browser permissions

AuraSolve asks Chrome for permission to read and fill the page you're on, save your preferences, and open its side panel. If a version also asks to use Chrome's debugger, that's only used to click buttons and dropdowns on homework sites that ignore ordinary extension clicks, and only while AuraSolve is filling an answer.

Children

AuraSolve isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Continuing to use AuraSolve after a change means you accept the updated policy.
