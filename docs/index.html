Questions, answered

Can I download AuraSolve right now?

Not yet. The Chrome extension isn’t public. Sign in to join the waiting list and we’ll email you when it’s available.

What is AuraSolve?

A Chrome extension that reads the question on your homework page, fills supported answer fields, and shows short step-by-step explanations. It works on Canvas classic quizzes, Google Forms, and Pearson MyLab.

Is AuraSolve free?

Every account starts with 5 free solves. After that, plans start at $0.99 for your first week. You sign in with just your email, and there's no API key to set up.

How do I cancel?

Open the AuraSolve panel, go to Account, and press Manage subscription. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome on a Mac or Windows computer. Other Chromium browsers like Edge or Brave may work, but Chrome is what it's built and tested for.

Does it get every question right?

No tool does. AuraSolve checks each answer against the question before filling it, but it can still miss, especially on blurry diagrams or unusual question types. Look over the steps it shows you.

Will it submit my assignment?

No. AutoPilot can press Check answer and move to the next question, but it never presses the final Submit assignment button. That's always your call.

Can I use it on graded work?

AuraSolve is made for practice. Follow your school's and instructor's rules about outside help on anything graded.

Where does it work?

Canvas classic quizzes, Google Forms, and Pearson MyLab. More platforms as we expand. It isn’t affiliated with Canvas, Google, or Pearson.

Is it made by Pearson?

No. AuraSolve is independent and isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Pearson, MyLab, Canvas, or Google.
